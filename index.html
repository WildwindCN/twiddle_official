01 / TwiddleSEED

Prototype

TwiddleSEED is the world's first hardware synthesizer with natural language timbre control. As the debut prototype powered by TwiddleAI's self-developed InspirationEngine 1.0, it bridges the gap between words and sound — turning text into playable timbres through an integrated display and responsive MIDI keyboard.

Natural Language Timbre Control

Describe any sound in words and hear it instantly. From technical specifications to poetic metaphors, your language becomes your instrument.

Integrated Display & Keyboard

A high-resolution screen meets a responsive MIDI keyboard, giving you direct visual and tactile control over every parameter.

Portable Creative Hub

A compact, all-in-one form factor designed for studios, stages, and everywhere inspiration strikes.

02 / InspirationEngine 1.0

InspirationEngine 1.0

Built on a Transformer architecture, InspirationEngine 1.0 is TwiddleAI's end-to-end natural language-to-timbre synthesis algorithm. It translates text into playable sound — from precise professional descriptors like "a bright, gritty, spacious vintage piano" to the poetic inspiration of "I wandered lonely as a cloud / That floats on high o'er vales and hills." This is not preset browsing. It is timbre creation born from language.

End-to-End Transformer

A self-developed neural network that understands both technical sound-design vocabulary and the emotional nuance of free-form language.

Dual-Mode Language Understanding

Handles rigorous professional descriptors and open-ended creative metaphors with equal fluency, capturing both precision and inspiration.

Real-Time Timbre Synthesis

Generate expressive, playable timbres instantaneously. No sampling libraries. No parameter knobs. Just words, transformed into sound.

Voice-Guided Incremental Editing

Tell TwiddleSEED "make it brighter" or "add more warmth" — the algorithm interprets your voice and refines the timbre in real time, one instruction at a time.
